# S4e — Catalogue stock summaries and alerts

Implement a read-only inventory slice using real S4d batches. Keep the current design and existing permission matrix; do not enable adjustments, valuation, allocation or checkout.

- Every product response returns stock_available=true, stock as the exact canonical sum of positive eligible batch atoms, and nearest_expiry as the earliest dated eligible batch or null. Eligibility excludes expired, quarantined and recalled batches and uses inclusive Africa/Dar_es_Salaam dates. No batches means real stock="0". Product active status remains independent: disabled products cannot be sold or newly received, but catalogue managers can inspect their retained balances.
- Owner/stockkeeper (receive_stock) additionally receive stock_total: signed exact sum of all held batch atoms, including expired/quarantined/recalled/negative balances. Selling staff receive the existing catalogue availability fields only, without total held quantities or costs. Do not mix whole items and kg across products or compute monetary valuation in this slice.
- Product filters low_stock=1 and expiring_in=N require receive_stock. Low stock is eligible stock <= reorder_level, including no-batch products and an exact equality boundary. expiring_in accepts 0..365 and matches a positive eligible dated batch between Tanzania today and today+N inclusive. Combine filters with existing search/category/department/barcode and paginate after filtering. low_stock=0 applies no low-stock restriction. Invalid values are 422; staff without the permission receive 403 when requesting these stock filters.
- Product list meta.low_stock_matches is provided only to stock staff and counts low-stock products matching the submitted filters, before pagination. The existing Stock value placeholder becomes Low-stock matches for stock staff; no valuation estimate is introduced.
- GET /stock/reorder-alerts uses receive_stock and returns paginated active, non-archived products at/below reorder level. GET /stock/expiry-alerts?days=30 uses receive_stock and returns paginated positive eligible batches of active, non-archived products dated today..today+days. days=0..365, default30; page/per_page follow 1..100/default50. Responses use the existing envelopes; expiry rows add product_name, unit and quantity_type to the batch resource. Cost remains permission-filtered.
- Sums must be exact MySQL integer/DECIMAL aggregates then checked against signed int64 before formatting. Unrepresentable persisted summaries return 409 STOCK_QUANTITY_OVERFLOW, never float/truncation. Online receiving checks both held and eligible aggregate bounds before adding a batch and rolls back overflowing receipts with 422 VALIDATION_ERROR. Keep signed stock columns for the future offline accept-and-flag policy; this adds no offline rejection rule.
- Summary queries and correlated filters use tenant-scoped ProductBatch models; endpoints return no-store even on errors. Receiving invalidates product queries so stock/filter/count data refresh from Laravel. Never persist inventory state in the browser.

Acceptance: exact counted/gram totals and carton shared stock; exclusion of expired/quarantined/recalled/negative batches; nearest eligible expiry; midnight/day-window boundaries; equality/no-stock filters and pagination/combined filters; inactive/archive handling; hidden costs/held totals/alerts for sellers; tenant leaks and overflow rollback; real UI empty/error/refresh behavior and desktop/phone receiving-to-catalogue refresh. No quantity or movement changes are made by reads.
