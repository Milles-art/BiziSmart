## Approved operating rules — D087

### Accounting and staff authority

- Owners manage supplier financial records, expenses and financial reports. Existing managers continue to have no buying-cost/financial-report access; stockkeepers may receive authorised goods but cannot set supplier balances or approve payments.
- Purchase orders reserve neither cash nor stock and create no debt. Accepted supplier-linked deliveries create supplier payable at their exact recorded cost; payments settle explicitly selected deliveries/invoices. No negative supplier balance; excess credits remain unapplied credits. Returned goods reduce stock immediately, while an expected credit reduces payable only once the owner records the supplier-issued credit note. No duplicate cost/stock receipt when matching invoice metadata.
- Expense entries distinguish occurrence date, paid/unpaid amount and payment source; adjustments are reversing journals, never silent edits/deletes. Owner approves expenses. Till-funded expenses require an open authorised till and bound approval; bank/mobile payments are records of externally completed payments, not transfers performed by BiziSmart.
- Cash drop records remove drawer cash immediately on cashier handover; assigned-branch managers/owners acknowledge receipt. Pending handovers remain visible and prevent closing the originating till until acknowledged or formally reversed; only owners resolve disputed handovers. Drops are internal transfers, never expenses/revenue deductions.
- Report sales/refunds/expense events are attributed to their own Tanzania local event dates. Accrual profit uses sale net revenue excluding snapshotted output VAT, original allocated batch COGS, returns and recorded expenses. Collection/payable payments affect cash flow, not revenue/expense twice. Show gross/net/VAT separately. Stock valuation shows eligible, expired/quarantined and total recorded stock separately using recorded batch cost; exclude unresolved negative offline quantities from physical valuation and flag them. Tax output is a bookkeeping summary of stored VAT snapshots, not a tax filing, TRA/EFD integration or assumed deductibility of supplier VAT.

### Pricing and later features

- Only owners configure schedules/promotions/markdowns; managers may approve checkout discounts within their existing branch scope but cannot see costs or configure margin alerts. Date windows use Tanzania local time. Apply at most one automatic offer per eligible item; choose the lowest valid resulting price and use a stable priority/ID tie break. Manual line/cart discounts need existing approvals and apply afterward. Buy-X-get-Y chooses lowest-priced eligible free units; never negative prices. Selected-batch markdowns cannot change expiry/quarantine eligibility or discount unrelated batches.
- Attendance is explicit clock-in/out with server timestamps, one open attendance session per staff member; owner reviews corrections with reasons. No GPS, biometric surveillance, automatic wage deduction or payroll calculation inferred. Notification preferences separate in-app/WhatsApp/SMS; missing external credentials leave external delivery unconfigured.
- Loyalty/commission rates are owner-configured and disabled until configured. Points accrue only on paid eligible value, with proportional return reversal; outstanding credit earns when collected. Commissions use collected net sales excluding VAT and refunds, recorded separately from wages; owner must approve payout records. Forecasts display the historical window, simple calculated demand and data limits; no invented AI accuracy claim or automated purchase.
- Offline selling uses the approved 24-hour device-authorisation window and accept-and-flag stock/price/expiry policy. Persist only required catalogue/queue data in a tenant/device-scoped store; clear unrelated sensitive data on logout, retain encrypted unsynced operations for safe recovery. Offline cash sales first; no claim of confirmed mobile payment without server verification. Offline credit/discounts/returns require their own signed limits/approval contracts before enabling those modes.
